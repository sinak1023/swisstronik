Invoice authenticity · Atomic USDC settlement

Invoices that prove themselves — and settle in one transaction.

Attestra writes each invoice directly onto Circle's Arc network: the file, its fingerprint, and an optional pay button all live on-chain. Anyone can verify an invoice is authentic and pay it in native USDC — with the platform fee split atomically by the contract. Your files never sit on our server.

Issue an invoice → Verify one

How it works

Three steps for the issuer, one scan for the customer.

Upload & seal

Your browser hashes the invoice, compresses it, and splits it into chunks. Nothing is uploaded to a server — bytes go straight to the contract.

Register on Arc

We show the exact USDC cost up front. On confirm, the file is stored on-chain via SSTORE2 and you get a verify link plus a QR code.

Verify & settle

Your customer scans the QR, sees the invoice rebuilt from chain data with a green “authentic” check, and can pay in USDC — settled instantly.

Why put it on-chain?

Invoice fraud — forged, altered, or double-paid invoices — is a costly, everyday problem. Arc is built for exactly this kind of financial settlement.

Tamper-proof

The original file's keccak256 fingerprint is sealed on-chain. Change a single pixel or digit and verification fails instantly.

Survives your server

Because the file itself is stored on Arc, the invoice stays verifiable even if your company or website goes away.

Atomic settlement

Payment and fee-split happen in a single transaction. Funds go straight from customer to issuer — the platform never custodies money.
